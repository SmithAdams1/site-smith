Modern Living in Lisbon's Innovation Hub

Located in Lisbon's rapidly developing Beato District, Urban Collection represents the perfect blend of contemporary design and urban convenience. This premium residential development offers 206 carefully designed units, each featuring modern amenities and thoughtful layouts that cater to today's lifestyle demands.

The development is strategically positioned in one of Lisbon's most promising neighborhoods, where innovation meets tradition, creating an exceptional investment opportunity in Portugal's thriving real estate market.

GUARANTEED 7% RETURN
Investors benefit from a guaranteed 7% annual return and co-ownership in a prime Lisbon development backed by Smith&Adams Group, a trusted name in real estate with over a decade of experience.
Full Property-Management
Full Property-Management means the Smith & Adams team in Lisbon handles your property end-to-end - cleaning, maintenance, guest communication, customer service, and pricing - so you earn income in euros passively while a professional in-house team responds quickly to any issue
Full Ownership
With Full Ownership, the apartment is entirely yours, in your name, within a Lisbon project designed to generate income and long-term appreciation in euros, combining legal security, direct exposure to the Portuguese real estate market, and specialized management by Smith & Adams.
