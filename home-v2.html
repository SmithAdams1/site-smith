How we can help

Three steps, one team.

    Understand your needs

    An initial consultation about what you want the asset to do: income, growth, residency, a home, or all of them.

  2. Step two

A typical client journey

From the first call to the resale, in one place.

  1. Investment advisoryStrategy, budget and location, including Golden Visa or D2 where residency is part of the plan.
  2. Due diligenceOur investment team and lawyers check the asset, the title and the numbers before you commit.
  3. Project managementFor properties under construction, we follow the works and the developer on your behalf.
  4. Handover and snaggingDefects listed and closed before the keys change hands.
  5. Asset managementFurnishing, licensing, condominium, utilities and maintenance.
  6. Rental managementShort or long-term letting, priced and run for occupancy and yield.
  7. ReportingIncome, costs and occupancy, in the same format every quarter.
  8. Exit and resaleWhen the time comes, we value, market and sell the asset for you.
