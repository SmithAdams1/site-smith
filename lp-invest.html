What you get

One property in Portugal, structured so it gives your family a European base, an income while you hold it and a defined way out.

  1. Residency for your entire family

    Your spouse or partner, dependent children and dependent parents can be included, subject to the current rules.

  2. Target 7% net yield

    The property is placed on an income mandate targeting a 7% net rental return a year, euro-denominated and reported to you. A projection, not a guarantee.*

  3. Freedom of travel

    Live in Portugal and travel visa-free across the Schengen Area.

  4. EU citizenship

    Residency is the route to permanent residency and then Portuguese, and so EU, citizenship after the qualifying years. The period is under reform in 2026; we confirm the current timeline for your case.

  5. Hands-off

    A passive route to a passport. We manage the property for you, so you have no involvement in running the asset.

  6. Easy exit

    A guaranteed buy-back at your original purchase price at year five, written into the contract, instead of depending on a future resale.

* €280,000 is an indicative entry level for eligible real estate; the applicable amount and qualifying residency route depend on your circumstances and are confirmed for your case against official sources (AIMA). The 7% is a projected net rental figure based on a conservative market projection, as of 2026, and is not guaranteed. The buy-back is a contractual undertaking at the original purchase price; capital is at risk. Information as of 2026 and general in nature; not legal, tax or investment advice. Residency and tax rules change.
