HYGGE
Concept

Hygge is an atmosphere and an experience, rather than about things. It is a calm, comfortable time with the people we love, a complete absence of frustrations and a total feeling of home.

At Smith & Adams, this philosophy is our guiding light. We design sanctuaries that invite you to slow down and take pleasure in savouring the moment.

HYGGE [hue-gah]

(n.) a calm, comfortable time with people you love. a complete absence of frustrations or anything emotionally overwhelming. taking pleasure from the cosy atmosphere and enjoying the simple pleasures in life. often enjoyed with good food, drinks, warm blankets and candlelight.

Hygge House
Hotel S. Julião

In the heart of historic Lisbon, the Hygge House Hotel in Rua de S. Julião is an extension of our beloved Kaffe concept into the world of hospitality.

This boutique project blends classic traditional Portuguese architecture with the soulful comfort of Hygge living.

Designed for travelers seeking a quiet refuge amidst the city's energy.

It is a destination where you don't just visit, you find a home away from home.

The hotel offers an intimate stay where the morning scent of specialty coffee and the cozy atmosphere of our Kaffe are always present.

A new way to stay in Lisbon

Nestled in the vibrant streets of downtown Lisbon, the Hygge House Hotel on Rua dos Fanqueiros is the natural extension of our brand into a new corner of the city.

With 30 thoughtfully designed units, this project carries the same mood, the same style and the same quality that define the Hygge experience, now elevated by a more refined architectural language.

Pure Comfort

Hygge House Beato blends modern architecture with urban convenience and the warm, welcoming spirit of the Hygge philosophy. The building comprises 137 studio units, designed for efficiency, functionality, and quality.

These units perfectly meet the high standards of Portugal's growing hospitality market. It is a project where long-term investment value meets a community focused on the genuine feeling of home, and the perfect choice for those seeking a modern refuge that stays connected to the city's historic roots and riverside energy.
