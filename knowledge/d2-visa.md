## VERIFIED (2026-08-29)
D2 (immigrant entrepreneur visa): NO fixed minimum investment - applicant proves funds to start/run the business + a viable business plan (market analysis, 3-5yr forecasts). Can create/buy/branch a company; no job-creation requirement. Visa valid ~4 months to enter PT, then convert to a 2-year residence permit at AIMA. Requires genuine residence (~6+ months/year). PR after 5 years; citizenship timeline in reform (verify). Sources: Global Citizen Solutions, Portugalist, imin-portugal (2026).

## VERIFIED (2026-08-30) - residence flexibility (Article 85)
Owner (Abilio) input: the D2 "genuine residence" requirement is softened by Article 85 of the Immigration Law (Lei n.º 23/2007), which governs cancellation of a residence permit for prolonged absence and ALLOWS exceptions where the absence is justified (e.g. professional/business reasons). This justified-absence exception is a large part of the D2's appeal for founders who must travel - it is not a rigid day-count. Frame accurately and keep the "confirm with a specialist" disclaimer; do not overstate (it is an exception for justified reasons, not an unconditional waiver).
