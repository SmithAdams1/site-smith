## VERIFIED (2026-09-08) - D2 "with Buy-Back" programme (source: /calculator, S&A site)
The offering behind the /lp-invest hook. Say these EXACTLY; the distinction is a compliance line.
- **Programme:** 5-year Portugal **D2 visa** (residency) built on an income-producing property. NOT Golden Visa (direct real estate stopped qualifying for GV in Oct 2023).
- **Buy-back = GUARANTEED, and it applies to CAPITAL:** "guaranteed buy back of the property at the original purchase price" at year 5. This is a contractual undertaking - the phrase "guaranteed buy-back" is S&A's own and may be used. It guarantees the CAPITAL exit, not the return.
- **7% = PROJECTED, NOT guaranteed:** the calculator labels it "7% rental income per year - Pessimist projection based on Market Projection". Always write it as "target/projected 7% net rental income", never "guaranteed 7%".
- **No acquisition costs:** "No property acquisition costs (IMT, Stamp Duty or notary) apply under the buy back structure."
- **Illustrative example (€300k property, family of 4):** 5-yr total cost ~€312,862; net rental income €21,000/yr x5 = €105,000; buy-back €300,000 (Y5); illustrative net profit ~€92,138; total ROI ~29.5%; average ~5.89%/yr. Label as illustrative, not a promise.
- **Mandatory disclaimer wording:** rental income is a projected figure (S&A conservative market projection, as of 2026), not guaranteed; the buy-back is a contractual undertaking at the original purchase price; capital is at risk; property/tax rules change; not legal/tax/investment advice.
- **Compliance note (US investors):** never state or imply guaranteed *returns*/yield to US persons. Guarantee language is confined to the buy-back (capital), phrased as contractual.
