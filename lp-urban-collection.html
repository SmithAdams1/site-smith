Residency & mobility

An investment that unlocks residency in Portugal

The €280,000 Urban Collection package is designed to qualify for Portugal's D2 Visa — one of the fastest and most secure routes to residency in the country. By investing, you can apply for a 5-year residence permit with the right to live in Portugal and travel visa-free across the Schengen Area.

Beyond the financial return, your investment becomes a strategic tool for global mobility and family planning — offering more security, quality of life, and flexibility for the future.

  • D2 Visa eligibility with the €280,000 investment package.
  • Initial 5-year residence permit, renewable under current law.
  • Visa-free Schengen travel while the permit is valid.
  • Family Reunification possible for relatives.
  • Potential pathway to Portuguese citizenship.
"More than an investment: a strategic route to European residency, with income in euros today and the possibility of citizenship tomorrow."
Location

Beato: where industrial heritage meets innovation

Urban Collection sits in Lisbon's Beato district — a riverside area rapidly transforming into one of the city's key hubs for innovation, culture, and entrepreneurship. Former industrial buildings are being converted into creative hubs, tech spaces, and new residential projects, driving demand for high-quality housing and long-term rentals.

For investors, this transformation creates a favourable environment: an emerging district with strong rental demand, lifestyle appeal for young professionals, and clear potential for capital appreciation as the neighbourhood matures.
