# Head of Strategy - Website Notes (transcribed 2026-08-29)

Source: "Notes on Website.pages" (Head of Strategy). Transcribed for the site improvement backlog. We work through this section by section as a team (Marketing, SEO, Web design, Head of Design, UX/UI).

## HomePage

### Nav bar suggestions
Proposed structure: Home / About Us / Invest in Portugal (generic page - the whole opportunity) / Our Services (dropdown: S&A Investment, S&A Immigration, S&A Real Estate, S&A Asset Management, S&A Rental Management, S&A Food & Beverage) / Contact Us.
Rationale: to elevate value and one day sell the individual functions, separate and define them; use the S&A group site for now, each can spin out into its own brand/site later. Names can change, but these are the defined areas of business.

### Design
- Get rid of the image/video walkthrough. Too large, 3-4s load, poor performance, worse on mobile + Google issues. "Performance is still too slow. NEW ONE IS EVEN WORSE."
- Nav bar goes white immediately when scrolling down, even though still on the video/scroll image.
- Logo is not dynamic - shrinks poorly when resizing the browser.
- CTA too invasive on first home page. Needs to be more subtle - "Learn more". Book/contact can be lower down.
- Fonts terrible. Too many styles/sizing. Want 2-3 size settings, ideally 1-2 fonts, same as the logo.
- Headline is a negative trying to spin positive. Never sell using negatives. Doesn't even make sense. Not a good headline.
- Stats poorly described. Numbers huge, sub-headers too small.
- Quoted a source that doesn't exist! Worse than no source.
- After the hero stats, segments look the same. No defined segmentation, hard to follow. Headers just standard lettering made bigger.
- Colour scheme too flat. Need a 3rd colour for emphasis. Currently blue/white + black/white text - dull, hard to read. Try Gold.
- No design consistency: click boxes some rounded, some squared. Pick one theme, identical throughout. Same for font types, colour, general structure - same everywhere.
- Footer not dynamic. Doesn't work well.
- Enormous empty gaps/boxes everywhere.

### AI Agent (chat widget)
Inaccurate advice: said rental management is only for clients who invested through us (false - we actively seek new properties to let); said we don't offer to sell your property, then said we focus on development. Needs better prompts and thorough checking.

- Hero first image: "We are independent advisory built around investor" - image of Suelen is good but poorly placed, wording covers her head. Header not dynamic, seems pinned to bottom of screen.

## Invest in Portugal
- First text segment just says "D2". No intro/reason/explanation. The site must answer questions, not create them. Begin with "Why invest in Portugal?" + overview of benefits (High Returns, EU Residency, Quality of Life, etc.).
- Then ~3 defined segments (all reiterating how we help):
  1. Objective Investment (working title) - the general reason to invest: ROI is high, tax incentives, etc.
  2. Residency by Investment - two sub-categories: Golden Visa and D2.
  3. Lifestyle - those who want the quality of life: holiday/retirement home.
- On each segment, a subtle CTA offering downloadable informative brochures: one for pure investors (Lisbon/Portugal strengths), for GV, D2, and lifestyle "find your new home".
