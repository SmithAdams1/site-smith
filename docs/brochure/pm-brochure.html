Why property management

Owning abroad is not hands-off by default.

Asset and property management. As a foreign owner you cannot easily set up water and electricity, wait in for a repair or chase a contractor. We handle all of it: utility contracts, a broken boiler, a leak, insurance claims and the paperwork. We represent you at condominium meetings and act as your tax representative where needed.

Rental management. We set prices by season and demand, manage listings on Airbnb, Booking.com and our own channel, look after guests or tenants from first message to check-out, and keep the home in a condition that earns good reviews. The aim: high occupancy, a strong net yield and an asset that holds its value.
