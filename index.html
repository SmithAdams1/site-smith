FAQs

Find Answers to Your Questions

For hundreds of successful clients, a new chapter began with us. We empower individuals and families to live, invest, and thrive in Portugal and other top global destinations.

Do I need to move to Portugal to apply for this program?
No, relocation is not required. You can complete the process remotely and visit only when needed for biometrics or document submission.

What is the minimum investment amount?
The minimum investment required is €280,000 in qualifying real estate in eligible areas, such as the S&A Urban Collection.

How long does the process take?
The process generally takes between 6 to 9 months to obtain the residency card, depending on the volume of applications at SEF/AIMA.

Can I include my family in the application?
Yes, Portugal's family reunification process allows you to include your spouse, dependent children, and dependent parents in the same application.

Is the rental income guaranteed?
Yes, properties in the S&A Urban Collection feature a guaranteed annual rental yield of 4% managed by our team.
