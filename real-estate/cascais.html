Property for sale in Cascais

Cascais is a coastal municipality west of Lisbon, connected to the city centre by the Cascais train line and the A5 motorway. It combines beaches, a marina and an established international community with easy access to Lisbon.

Why invest in Cascais

Coast and capital together

Owners get a seaside lifestyle while staying within commuting distance of Lisbon by train or car.

An international community

Cascais has long attracted foreign residents and families, supported by several international schools in the municipality and nearby.

A family-home market

Demand is led by people who live here, not only by visitors, which favours long-term rentals and owner-occupiers.

A premium price level

Cascais is one of the most expensive housing markets in Portugal, so entry costs are higher and gross yields tend to be lower than in Lisbon's more affordable districts.

Questions about buying in Cascais

How much does property cost in Cascais?

According to INE, the median value of homes sold in Cascais (municipality) was €4,889 per square metre in 12 months to June 2026. Asking prices in the 226 properties we list in Cascais run from €335,000 to €11,200,000, with a median of €2,100,000 (as of 26 Oct 2026).

What taxes do I pay when buying property in Cascais?

Buyers pay property transfer tax (IMT) and stamp duty of 0.8% on the purchase price. From 1 January 2026, IMT on a home that is not your main residence starts at 1% and rises through bands, up to a single 7.5% rate above €1,150,853. Since May 2026, non-resident buyers of housing pay a single 7.5% IMT rate, unless they were tax resident in Portugal before or become resident within two years. Each property page has a calculator.

Does buying property in Cascais qualify for the Golden Visa?

No. The direct real-estate route to the Golden Visa closed in October 2023. As of 2026 the Golden Visa is obtained through other investments, such as regulated investment funds. Buying a home can still support a residence application under other visas, such as the D2 for entrepreneurs. We advise on both.
