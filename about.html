Who we are

Smith & Adams Group

Smith & Adams is a Lisbon-based real estate investment advisory firm. We give investors an independent view of the Portuguese property market, then go further: coordinating an end-to-end solution across planning, development, construction, and property management, so a single advisory relationship covers the full lifecycle of the investment.

Our focus is simple: help objective investors maximise the return on any tangible asset they acquire, while keeping ownership genuinely hands-off. From the first conversation to ongoing management, everything we do is built around that outcome.

What we do

Advisory, residency and management

Investment Advisory & Structuring

We identify and structure real estate investment opportunities in Portugal, advising on asset selection, legal and tax framework, financing, and long-term strategy, for investors focused on capital preservation, euro income, and steady growth.

Residency & Citizenship Advisory

We coordinate immigration and legal services for clients pursuing Golden Visa or D2 Visa residency, working alongside specialised law firms and regulated partners to manage the visa process, property transaction, and family inclusion end to end.

Asset & Property Management

For properties we’ve advised on, and for independent owners, we manage every asset to maximise its yield potential, backed by a proven track record of over 90% occupancy across our short-term rental portfolio. From tenant relations and maintenance to financial reporting, we handle the day-to-day so ownership from abroad stays genuinely hands-off.

A message from our Head of Investment

We built Smith & Adams around one idea: that you should be able to own property in Portugal without having to become an expert in Portugal.

Most investors arrive with the same two questions — is this a good asset, and who looks after it once I have bought it? Answering only the first is what the market usually offers. We give an independent read on the Portuguese property market and then stay on: planning, development, construction and management sit inside the same relationship, so nobody hands you off at the point where the work actually starts.

Portugal earns the attention. It offers residency routes into the European Union for both entrepreneurs and passive investors, a legal system that treats foreign ownership the same as domestic, and cities where the demand for good, well-run housing has outrun the supply of it. That combination is rarer than it sounds.

What we ask you to judge us on is evidence rather than argument. More than 1,000 investors, over €300M placed and 300+ assets under management — and, on this site, the occupancy of our own buildings with the dates and the source attached. If a number matters to your decision, you should be able to see where it came from.

George Hobson

Head of Investment & Strategy, Smith & Adams
