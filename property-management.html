We treat your property as a true investment asset.

We manage the daily operations of your apartment already listed on rental platforms - ensuring professional management and consistent euro-denominated income with complete peace of mind.

How it works

  • 01Property integrationWe connect your apartment to our operational systems, integrating it with the rental platforms you already use.
  • 02Day-to-day tenant managementWe handle all tenant relations: communication, check-ins, check-outs, and issue resolution.
  • 03Maintenance & asset careWe coordinate cleaning and maintenance teams, perform regular inspections, and monitor the overall condition of your property.
  • 04Financial control & reportingWe manage payments and recurring expenses and send you clear monthly reports - track performance from anywhere.

Who it's for

Smith & Adams investors

Owners of units in our Hygge House Projects, whose daily operations are managed by our specialised team.

Independent property owners

Those who already have an apartment listed on rental platforms and want to delegate operational management to a professional company based in Lisbon.

Asset Management

Full-cycle care for your Lisbon property.

Beyond day-to-day lettings, our Asset Management service is a comprehensive solution designed to protect your investment, keep it fully compliant, and maximise its long-term value - so ownership stays hands-off wherever you are.

Property health checks & investment reviews

Comprehensive inspections with detailed reports, photographs and condition assessments. Regular reviews catch issues early and keep your property market-ready.

Asset preservation & maintenance

We oversee all maintenance and repairs, coordinating trusted contractors on your behalf. A proactive approach minimises unexpected costs and preserves long-term value.

Key holding & emergency response

Secure key holding with rapid response for emergencies, inspections and contractor access - complete peace of mind, wherever you are.

Condominium representation

We act as your local representative for all condominium matters: attending meetings, liaising with management companies, handling annual levies and keeping you informed of decisions.

Insurance assistance & claims

We help source suitable insurance for your property and provide hands-on support through any claims, protecting both the asset and your rental income.

Rental & compliance support

Guidance on regulation, documentation, licensing and compliance for long-term, medium-term or short-term rentals - keeping your investment protected and legally sound.

Tax representation

Your designated tax representative in Portugal: we handle IMI and rental-income correspondence, register lease agreements and receipts, submit annual returns, and issue certificates when needed.

Investment enhancement strategy

Tailored recommendations on upgrades, furnishings, energy efficiency and market positioning - to lift resale value and occupancy, reduce maintenance costs and protect ROI.
