Complete Property Management for your real estate in Portugal

The Smith & Adams Group manages 100% of your apartment’s operations, so you can enjoy steady euro‑denominated income, effortlessly and from anywhere.

We take care of your property as a true investment asset.

We manage the daily operations of your apartment already listed on rental platforms, ensuring professional management and consistent euro‑denominated income with complete peace of mind.

How it works – 4 steps

1

Property integration

We connect your apartment to our operational and reporting systems, integrating it with the rental platforms you already use.

2

Day‑to‑day tenant management

We handle all tenant relations: communication, check‑ins and check‑outs, and issue resolution.

3

Maintenance, cleaning & asset care

We coordinate cleaning and maintenance teams, perform regular inspections, and monitor the overall condition of your property.

4

Financial control & reporting

We manage payments and recurring expenses and send you clear reports, so you can track your property’s performance from anywhere.
